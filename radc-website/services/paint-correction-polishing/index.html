What is paint correction?

Paint correction is the process of machine-polishing your car's paint to remove defects such as swirl marks, holograms, light scratches, water-spot etching and oxidation. Instead of hiding marks with fillers, correction levels the clear coat so light reflects evenly — which is what creates that wet, mirror-like gloss.

1-step, 2-step or 3-step: what's the difference?

  • 1-Step polish — a single all-in-one polish. Ideal for newer cars, or paint that's dull but not badly damaged. Big gloss improvement for the price.
  • 2-Step polish — a cutting stage to remove moderate defects, followed by a finishing polish to restore clarity.
  • 3-Step polish — our most thorough correction: heavier cut, refine and jewel finish. Best for older vehicles, dark colours and show cars.
  • Flat & polish — wet-sanding (flatting) to level deeper defects, orange peel or runs, followed by polishing.

Not sure which you need? Send us photos on WhatsApp in direct sunlight — swirls show up clearly — and we'll recommend the right level.

What's included

Why paint fades so fast in Oudtshoorn

Intense Klein Karoo sun breaks down clear coat over time, leaving reds pink, blacks grey and older single-stage paint chalky. Dust wiped off dry, rough car-wash brushes and gravel roads add swirls. The good news: most of this damage sits in the top layer and can be polished out.

Protect your corrected paint

Once your paint is corrected, protect it. Upgrading to a polish + ceramic package costs less than doing the two separately and keeps the finish looking fresh for months or years.

Frequently asked questions

How much does car polishing cost in Oudtshoorn?

At RADC a 1-step polish starts at R1 000 for a sedan or hatch, 2-step at R1 300 and 3-step at R1 699.99. SUVs, minibuses and double cabs start at R1 500, R2 000 and R2 500 respectively.

Will polishing remove all scratches?

Polishing removes swirls and scratches in the clear coat. A scratch you can catch with your fingernail has likely gone through the clear coat and may need touch-up or a repair — see our dent & scratch repair service.

Is machine polishing safe for my paint?

Yes, when done properly. Correction removes only a tiny amount of clear coat, and we choose the least aggressive method that achieves the result.

Can you polish a minibus or double-cab bakkie?

Yes — we have dedicated SUV, minibus and double-cab polishing packages.
