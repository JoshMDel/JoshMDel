What is a ceramic coating?

A ceramic coating is a liquid protective layer that bonds to your car's clear coat and cures into a hard, glossy, water-repellent shell. Compared with a traditional wax, it lasts far longer, makes washing much easier (dirt and water slide off), and adds a layer of defence against UV fading, light chemical etching and environmental fallout.

A coating is not a force-field — it won't stop stone chips or deep scratches — but on a well-maintained car it keeps the paint looking freshly polished for a long time.

Why every package includes a polish

A coating locks in whatever is underneath it — including swirl marks, water spots and oxidation. That's why RADC pairs every ceramic package with a 1-step, 2-step or 3-step machine polish:

  • 1-Step + ceramic — for newer cars or paint in good condition that needs a gloss boost before coating.
  • 2-Step + ceramic — a cutting stage plus a refining stage for moderate swirls and dullness.
  • 3-Step + ceramic — full paint correction for older, faded or heavily swirled paint before protection.

12-month-plus vs 5-year ceramic: which should you choose?

Choose a 12-month-plus package if you want an affordable gloss and protection boost, are planning to sell the car, or simply want to try a coating. Choose a 5-year package if you plan to keep the vehicle, park outside a lot, or regularly drive gravel and coastal roads — the longer-life coating spreads the cost over years of easier maintenance.

Why ceramic protection makes sense in the Klein Karoo

Oudtshoorn's long, hot summers and high UV levels slowly oxidise unprotected paint, especially reds, blacks and older single-stage finishes. Add fine Karoo dust and the salt air you pick up on trips to Mossel Bay or George, and paint ages quickly. A coating keeps contamination from bonding, so a simple wash restores the shine.

Ceramic coating aftercare

  • Avoid washing the car for the first few days while the coating fully cures.
  • Hand wash with a pH-neutral shampoo and clean microfibre — avoid brush-type car washes.
  • Remove bird droppings and bugs promptly.
  • Book a ceramic treatment wash periodically to top up the hydrophobic layer.

Frequently asked questions

How much is ceramic coating in Oudtshoorn?

RADC ceramic packages start at R1 299.99 for a 1-step polish plus 12-month-plus ceramic on a sedan or hatch. 5-year packages run from R4 299 (sedan/hatch) to R6 000 (SUV/bakkie with 2-step polish).

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Plan to leave your car with us for the day. Larger vehicles and 3-step correction packages can take longer — we'll confirm timing when you book.

Can you ceramic coat a bakkie or SUV?

Yes. We have dedicated SUV, double-cab bakkie and minibus ceramic packages priced for the larger surface area.

Does ceramic coating protect against stone chips?

No. A ceramic coating protects against UV, contamination and light marring and makes washing easier, but it does not stop stone chips. Consider paint protection film for high-impact areas.
