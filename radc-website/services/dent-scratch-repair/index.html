Dent, Scratch & Rim Repair in Oudtshoorn

Car-park scrapes, faded panels, kerbed rims or a bumper that's popped loose — RADC repairs the small things that make a car look tired, right up to full exterior resprays.

Repairs we handle

  • Scratch & faded-paint removal (from R300) — removing light scratches and small dents and reviving faded or chalky panels.
  • Minor dent repairs (from R1 600) — removing small dents with a respray of the affected area.
  • Rim repairs (from R400) — kerb rash and rim restoration to make wheels look right again.
  • Bumper alignment (R200) — re-seating and aligning loose or misaligned bumpers after a bump.
  • Full body respray (from R13 500) — a full exterior respray with materials included.

Get an accurate quote fast

Every repair is different, so the quickest way to a firm price is to WhatsApp us clear photos:

  • One photo from about 2 m away so we can see the panel.
  • One close-up of the damage, ideally in daylight.
  • Your vehicle's make, model and colour.

Repair, then protect

After paintwork, ask about combining your repair with a machine polish so the repaired area blends with the rest of the car, or a ceramic package to keep it looking new.

Frequently asked questions

How much does scratch removal cost in Oudtshoorn?

Scratch and faded-paint removal starts at R300. Deeper scratches that need paintwork are quoted from photos.

Do you repair rims?

Yes, rim repairs start at R400 per job depending on the damage.

How much is a full respray?

A full exterior respray starts at R13 500 including materials. Colour changes and extensive bodywork are quoted separately.

Can you fix a loose bumper?

Yes — bumper alignment is R200 where clips and brackets are intact.
