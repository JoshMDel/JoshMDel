Choose the right clean

  • Basic wash & vacuum (R130) — pre-wash, hand wash and interior vacuum for regular upkeep.
  • Ceramic treatment wash (R260) — our basic wash plus a ceramic gloss enhancer that leaves paint slick and water-repellent. Great for maintaining coated cars.
  • Decon wash & clay bar (R217) — a decontamination/acid wash and clay bar to pull out bonded grime, rail dust and overspray that a normal wash leaves behind. Paint feels smooth as glass afterwards.
  • Engine bay detailing (R150) — engine bay cleaned and dressed. Ideal before a sale or service.
  • Full valet (from R1 000) — a complete interior and exterior valet for sedans and hatches, from R1 399 for SUVs, double cabs and minibuses.

Why a proper hand wash matters

Automatic brushes and dirty sponges drag Karoo dust across your paint and cause the swirl marks that make cars look dull. A careful hand wash with a pre-wash stage loosens grit first, so far less of it gets rubbed into the clear coat.

Full valet: ideal for

  • Selling your car — a valeted car photographs better and sells faster.
  • After a family holiday to the coast, or a season of farm and gravel roads.
  • Taxis, shuttles and minibuses that need a regular deep clean.
  • Spotless interiors — a "spotless interior" is something our Google reviewers mention.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a car wash at RADC in Oudtshoorn?

A basic wash and vacuum is R130. A ceramic treatment wash is R260 and a decon wash with clay bar is R217.

How much is a full valet?

A full valet is R1 000 for a sedan or hatch and R1 399 for SUVs, double-cab bakkies and minibuses.

What does a clay bar do?

A clay bar lifts contaminants bonded to the paint surface that washing can't remove, leaving it smooth. It's also the essential first step before polishing or coating.

Do you clean minibuses and taxis?

Yes. We have valet pricing for SUVs, double cabs and minibuses — WhatsApp us for regular or fleet bookings.
