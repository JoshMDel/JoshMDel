About Ryall's Auto Detailing Centre

A local, owner-run detailing centre in Colridge View, Oudtshoorn — built on passion for cars, honest pricing and the support of our community.

Cleaner. Shinier. Protected.

Ryall's Auto Detailing Centre — RADC for short — was started by Oudtshoorn local Ryall Wagenaar out of a genuine love for cars and for making them look their best. You can see that passion in the Bordeaux Honda Civic that features across our social media.

We're a premium vehicle detailing service that specialises in restoring showroom shine. From minor scratch removal to complete paint restoration, we use proper machine-polishing techniques and equipment to bring out the best in your vehicle's paintwork. We don't just wash cars — we meticulously detail and polish every surface to reveal a deeper, richer gloss.

What we stand for

  • Your support matters — RADC has grown thanks to the loyalty and word-of-mouth of local customers.
  • Clean cars, happier people — we treat every vehicle as if it were our own.
  • Honest, upfront pricing — every package is published.
  • From our family to yours — a local business serving local families, with faith at its core: “Everything through Christ our Lord.” (Philippians 4:13)
