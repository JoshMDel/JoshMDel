Why Oudtshoorn is the ideal hub

Oudtshoorn lies in the Garden Route District (previously the Eden District Municipality) at the heart of the Klein Karoo. Three mountain passes — Outeniqua, Robinson and Meiringspoort/Swartberg — link it to George, Mossel Bay and the Central Karoo, so most of the region is within an easy drive.

Because services like paint correction and ceramic coating are long-lasting investments, one trip to RADC gives you months or years of results.
