Paint care challenges in the Klein Karoo

Klein Karoo vehicles work hard: gravel farm roads, fine red dust, summer heat over 35 °C and intense UV. The result is faded paint, chalky plastics, yellow headlights and dust-packed interiors. A full valet takes care of the dust; a headlight restoration restores night visibility on dark country roads; and a ceramic package protects the paint from the sun.

Planning your trip to RADC

Dysselsdorp is about 20 minutes away, De Rust about 30 minutes on the N12, Calitzdorp about 40 minutes on the R62, and Ladismith and Uniondale a little over an hour. Combine your drop-off with shopping or appointments in Oudtshoorn.
