Paint care challenges in Mossel Bay

Salt-laden sea air settles on paint, chrome and rims, and once it bonds it accelerates oxidation and corrosion. Combine that with the reflected glare of beach days and paint dulls fast. A decon wash and clay bar removes bonded salt and fallout, and a ceramic coating stops it bonding again so a simple rinse keeps the car clean.

Frequently asked questions

How far is RADC from Mossel Bay?

About 85 km via the R328 Robinson Pass — roughly an hour's drive.

Does ceramic coating help with sea air?

Yes. A coating creates a slick barrier that keeps salt and fallout from bonding to the clear coat, making it much easier to wash off. Regular rinsing is still important.
